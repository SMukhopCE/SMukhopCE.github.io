Sudarshana Mukhopadhyay

Data Scientist and Statistician working on statistical hydrology, climate change, and water resources management.

Sudarshana is a former Postdoctoral Employee with Morgan C Levy at Scripps Institution of Oceanography, UC San Diego.

She earned her Ph.D. in Spring 2020 from the Department of Civil, Construction and Environmental Engineering at NCSU. She worked as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Climate, Hydrology and Water Resources: Modeling and Synthesis Group with Dr. Sankar Arumugam. She previously worked as a postdoc at the Steinschneider Lab at Cornell University.
